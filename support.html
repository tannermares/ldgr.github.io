Common questions

Does Ldgr connect to my bank?

No, and that's on purpose. You add every purchase by hand. That little bit of effort is what keeps you on top of what's left, instead of finding out after the fact.

How much does it cost?

Ldgr is free. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Do I need an iCloud account?

Not to use it on one device. You only need iCloud if you want your budgets to sync across your devices or you want to share a budget with someone.

How does sharing a budget work?

You can share any budget with one person or a few through iCloud. Everyone you invite sees that budget's purchases, and each purchase shows who added it, so you always know who spent what.

Can budgets reset automatically?

Yes. Each budget resets on the schedule you pick (daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly), and you can page back through past periods to see how you did.

Is my financial data private?

Yes. Your budgets and purchases live in your own iCloud account, and only you and the people you invite can see them (not even me). There are no analytics and no ads. The privacy policy has all the details.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete any budget or purchase in the app whenever you want. Since everything is stored in your iCloud account, you can also manage it from your Apple Account and iCloud settings.

Still stuck?

Email support@ldgrapp.com with what happened and what you expected. If it's a bug, your device and iOS version help a lot too.
